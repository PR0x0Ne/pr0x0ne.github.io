⚙️ How gasgauge Works

Every Ethereum transaction consumes gas, and the price of that gas is set by how crowded the network is at that moment. When many people are sending transactions at once, the price climbs. When the network is quiet, it drops. gasgauge reads public network information and turns it into numbers you can read at a glance, without you needing to understand the mechanics underneath.

The app does not connect to your wallet, does not hold any funds, and does not ask for private keys or seed phrases. It only reads publicly available network data. That means there is no risk to your crypto holdings from simply having gasgauge open.

Under the EIP-1559 model used by Ethereum today, a transaction fee is made up of a base fee that the network burns and a priority tip that goes to whoever processes the transaction. gasgauge separates these two numbers so you can see the difference between what the network charges and what you are choosing to add as an incentive.
